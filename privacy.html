1 Who We Are

TagsAI ("we", "us", "our") operates the TagsAI website located at yourdomain.com (the "Service"). We provide a free AI-powered YouTube video tag generation tool to help content creators improve their video discoverability.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our Service. By using TagsAI, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.

2 Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use our Service:

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use the tag generator tool, you enter text such as video titles, keywords, topics, or descriptions. This text is transmitted to the AI API (Anthropic Claude) to generate relevant tags. We do not permanently store this input on our servers.

If you contact us via our Contact form, we collect your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content

This information is used only to respond to your enquiry.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type, browser version, pages visited, time and date of your visit, time spent on pages.
  • Device information: Device type (mobile/desktop/tablet), operating system, screen resolution.
  • Usage data: How you interact with the generator tool (e.g., number of tag generations), which buttons you click, and general navigation patterns.

This data is collected in aggregated, anonymized form and is used only to improve the website.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect payment information (this tool is free).
  • We do not collect your YouTube account information.
  • We do not require account registration.
  • We do not collect your generated tags after they are displayed.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the TagsAI Service.
  • Generate relevant YouTube tags based on your input using the AI API.
  • Respond to your enquiries and support requests.
  • Understand how users interact with our tool so we can improve it.
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends and technical performance.
  • Detect and prevent technical issues, abuse, or misuse of the Service.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

We will never use your information for unsolicited marketing, spam, or sell it to any third party.

4 AI Processing & Your Inputs

When you click "Generate Tags", the text you enter is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate YouTube tags. Please be aware of the following:

  • Your input is transmitted securely to Anthropic's servers for AI processing.
  • Anthropic processes your input to generate tag suggestions and returns the results to us.
  • We do not store your input text after the tags have been generated and returned to you.
  • Anthropic may use API inputs to improve their models unless you opt out through their API settings. We recommend reviewing Anthropic's Privacy Policy for their specific data handling practices.
  • Do not enter sensitive personal information, confidential business information, or private data into the tag generator input field.
Important: Only enter video-related information such as titles, topics, and keywords. Do not enter passwords, financial details, personal identification information, or any sensitive private data.

5 Cookies & Analytics

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences (e.g., language settings).
  • Analyze how users interact with our website using anonymized analytics data.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for the website to function properly. Session
Analytics Understand how visitors use our website (anonymized). Up to 2 years
Preference Remember your settings and preferences. Up to 1 year

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6 Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate TagsAI:

Anthropic Claude API AI Engine

Used to generate YouTube tags from your input. Your input text is transmitted to Anthropic for processing.

Anthropic Privacy Policy ↗
Web Hosting Provider Infrastructure

Our website is hosted on a third-party server. Server logs may include IP addresses and basic request data.

Analytics Service Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Plausible or Fathom) to understand site usage in an anonymized way.

We do not share your personal data with any third parties for advertising, marketing, or commercial purposes.

7 Data Retention

We retain different types of data for different periods:

  • Tag generator inputs: Not stored. Inputs are sent to the AI API and discarded after tags are returned.
  • Contact form submissions: Retained for up to 12 months to allow follow-up communication, then deleted.
  • Server logs: Retained for up to 90 days for security and performance analysis, then deleted automatically.
  • Analytics data: Retained in aggregated, anonymized form for up to 24 months.

You may request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us. See Section 12 for contact details.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of personal data we hold about you.

Right to Correction

Request correction of inaccurate personal data.

Right to Deletion

Request deletion of personal data we hold about you.

Right to Object

Object to processing of your personal data.

Right to Portability

Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

Right to Restrict

Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

9 Children's Privacy

TagsAI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such information.

10 Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our website.
  • Limited access to personal data by our team members.
  • Regular review of our data collection and storage practices.
  • Using established third-party providers with strong security practices.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

12 Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

privacy@yourdomain.com
Online — Contact Form: Contact Us

We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 5 business days.
